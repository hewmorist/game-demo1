Playing the puzzle

First, use the Bombe to recover the secret. Each letter occurrence has its own squiggle, so repeated letters are hidden. After solving the secret, resolve one first-use and one repeat in staging to uncover the letter substitution underneath.

Tap a symbol to load it into the search machine. After completing staging, press Decode Squiggles and tap a symbol to guess its plaintext letter. Spaces and punctuation are visible. Symbols may be rotated or mirrored; all eight orientations mean the same letter. Use the Bombe choice table, then match its selected shape by eye against the alphabet. Before decoding, tapping loads the machine directly; dragging works too.

Using the Bombe

Select a puzzle symbol to load the machine. Compare it with each trial, then stop at the exact 100% peak.

Run pauses 20 ms between trials. Drag the slider to jump to any trial. Hold Next for fast scanning; release to stop.

The machine replays the daily sequence of six-arm trials and compares all eight rotations and reflections. Correlation measures ink similarity, not the probability of a correct clue. The machine dings at high correlation and keeps running. Stop it, then use the slider or Previous/Next to position it at 100%. Choose the peak number and letter from the table to add it to your anagram. Each position in the puzzle earns one letter, even when its symbol repeats. The position changes the trial order. Confirmed letters appear in anagram order; repeated scans of the same position earn nothing extra.

Run the Bombe and stop at the exact peak. Then choose that number and its squiggle from the table. Your selection goes into the comparator. Find the matching shape in the daily alphabet; rotate or flip to align it, then Check alignment to resolve all positions in this group.

The graph zooms into correlations from 50% to 100%, displayed as 0 to 100. Values below 50% are outside the graph. The matching calculation and exact 100% peak are unchanged.

Using Staging

The alphabet is shown below, followed by the message. ○ marks first-use symbols; ↻ marks repeats. Choose any position from either group to load the Bombe and its choice table. Run the machine, stop at the exact peak, and select its number and squiggle. Find the matching alphabet shape in the comparator and Check alignment. One successful first-use resolves all first-use symbols; one successful repeat resolves all repeats. Work in either order. If a table selection does not match, run the Bombe again. When both groups are solved, press Decode Squiggles.

Drag an alphabet symbol into the left box and a puzzle symbol into the right box. On a phone, tap an alphabet symbol, then tap a puzzle position. Flip or rotate the right symbol to check the alignment.
