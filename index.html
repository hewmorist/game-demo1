Load a puzzle symbol to begin.

The machine replays the daily sequence of six-arm trials and compares all eight rotations and reflections. Correlation measures ink similarity, not the probability of a correct clue. The machine dings at high correlation and keeps running. Stop it, then use the slider or Previous/Next to position it at 100%. Press Get secret letter to claim an exact match. Each position in the puzzle earns one letter, even when its symbol repeats. The position changes the trial order. Confirmed letters appear in anagram order; repeated scans of the same position earn nothing extra.
